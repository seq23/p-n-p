What birthday party planning includes

Event planning for parties covers two different jobs. One is the look and feel of the room. The other is vendor management: caterers, entertainers, rentals, and a minute-by-minute timeline. Porch & Party plans the first job completely and is upfront about the second.

  • Theme and color direction. One look built around the guest of honor, their age, and the room.
  • Layout. Where the focal point goes, where guests gather, where the cake and gifts sit, and where photos will be taken, based on the actual space.
  • Balloon and backdrop design. Balloon garlands, arches, and backdrop walls sized to the room, when included in scope.
  • Table and dessert styling. Table moments, signage, and a styled dessert or grazing area so the food looks like part of the plan.
  • On-site setup. The decor is delivered and installed before guests arrive, so the host is not up on a ladder the morning of the party.
  • Hotel coordination. For hotel-room parties, timing is planned around check-in, room access, and the hotel’s decor rules.

What is not included

Porch & Party does not book caterers, DJs, entertainers, or venues, and it does not rent tents, tables, chairs, or linens. If your party needs those, the quote reply will tell you what to line up and how the decor plan fits around it. For grazing tables, styling is included and food sourcing can be added for an additional fee.

Kids birthday parties

Kids birthdays work best with one clear idea that the child recognizes the moment they walk in: a favorite color, a character-free theme like space or garden, or a milestone number. The plan usually puts the effort into one strong focal wall or corner with a balloon garland and a styled dessert table, then carries two or three colors through the rest of the room. Setups are planned for real use, with balloons kept clear of walkways and room left to play.

Adult and milestone birthdays

Adult birthdays tend to lean on mood rather than theme. A 30th, 40th, or 50th might call for a neutral and gold palette, an oversized number display, candles and florals on the table, and a backdrop built for group photos. Surprise parties need a setup window that fits around the guest of honor’s schedule, so share the timing and the person who will let the team in.

Hotel-room birthday parties

Hotel-room birthdays are one of the most requested setups in Memphis because they combine a getaway with a surprise. The plan focuses on what the guest sees first: the entry, the bed, and one photo spot, with balloons, lighting, and a small table moment. Setup depends on the hotel’s rules about decor and room access, so include the hotel name or area, the check-in time, and when the guest of honor will arrive. Can you plan a kids birthday party at home?

Yes. Home birthday setups for kids are planned around the age group, the theme, and the room or yard you have. The plan usually centers on one strong focal area, such as a balloon backdrop and dessert table, so the party looks finished without decorating every wall.

Can you set up a birthday surprise in a hotel room?

Yes, when the hotel allows decor access before arrival. Share the hotel name or area, the date, and the arrival time. Porch & Party plans the room around the bed, entry, and one photo spot, and confirms timing with the hotel rules before setup day.

How far ahead should I book a birthday setup?

Two to three weeks ahead is comfortable for most birthdays, and earlier for weekends in spring and December. Short-notice requests are still reviewed; availability depends on the date and the size of the setup.
