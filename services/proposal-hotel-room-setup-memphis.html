What does a proposal hotel room setup include?

  • A rose-petal path from the door to where you will ask
  • LED candles along the path and around the room
  • A "Marry Me" sign or marquee-style letters
  • Balloons in white, red or gold, and a petal heart on the bed
  • A spot for the photo, planned around the window and the light

Tell us where in the room you want to kneel and we build the room toward it.

How much does a proposal hotel room setup cost?

Basic starts at $300, Luxury at $500 and Signature at $1,500. A proposal usually sits in Luxury or Signature, because the candles and the sign do most of the work.

How do I plan the day so it stays a secret?

  1. Book the room and send us your date and colors. No hotel booked yet? Leave the hotel blank on the request form and text it to us once you have it.
  2. Check in early and arrange access with the front desk, or meet us at the room.
  3. Take them to dinner. We are usually at the hotel between 3 and 6 PM, around check-in time, and gone before your person walks in.
  4. We text you a photo when it is ready. Walk in, and ask.

What happens after the yes?

Stay the night in it. We use LED candles only, never an open flame, so nothing sets off a smoke detector or breaks a hotel rule. Takedown is optional: for a hotel room it is $75, and it is included with Signature.

FAQ

What do people ask about a proposal hotel room setup?

Will you be there when I propose?

No. We finish the room and leave before you arrive, so the moment is just the two of you. We text you a photo when it is ready.

Can you write our names or a message?

Yes. A "Marry Me" sign is the classic; we can also do lettering, a banner or your names. Tell us on the request form.

Are the candles real?

No. We use LED candles only, never an open flame. They look real in photos and keep you inside hotel fire and smoke-detector rules.

How far ahead should I book?

Two to three weeks is comfortable; earlier for Valentine's Day, Christmas and New Year's Eve weekends. If your date is sooner, send the request and we will tell you within one business day.

Do you take it down?

You keep it, or we come back for takedown: optional, $75 for a hotel room, and included with Signature.
