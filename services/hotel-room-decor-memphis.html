Romantic and anniversary hotel room setups

A heart of red rose petals on the bed, candles on the nightstands, white, gold or red balloons by the headboard and a bouquet on the pillows: the classic romantic room, for an anniversary, a getaway night or "just because". To match colors, send a photo or a Pinterest link with your request.

Proposal hotel room decor

A path of rose petals and candles from the door to the bed, roses waiting where the question gets asked. We set it up while you are out and are gone before the two of you walk in.

Birthday hotel room decor

A balloon garland over the headboard, gifts and a small bouquet on the bed and soft lights: a birthday surprise for a staycation or a night out in Memphis.

Hotel access, timing and price

FAQ

Hotel Room Setups questions

Can you decorate a hotel room before we arrive?

Yes. For a hotel we coordinate access with the front desk or with you. One to three hours depending on the setup. We arrive early enough to be gone before guests, or before your person walks in.

How much does a hotel room setup cost?

From $300+. The total depends on the size of the room, the pieces (balloons, backdrop, florals) and how many tables. Tell us your budget range on the form and we design to it.

What can a hotel room setup include?

Birthdays, anniversaries, proposals, baby and bridal showers, welcome-home surprises, hotel-room surprises and parties with a grazing table. Balloon arches and garlands, backdrops, rose petals and candles, table styling and signs.

How far ahead should I book?

Two to three weeks ahead is comfortable for most anniversaries and birthdays, and earlier for weekends in spring and December.

Do you take it down afterwards?

You keep it, or we come back for takedown. Takedown starts at $75, quoted with your setup.

How do I pay?

Your date is held by 50% of the quote, paid by Zelle or Cash App from your invoice. The rest is due by the time we arrive to set up. Zelle or Cash App only; we do not take cash or cards.
