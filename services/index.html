Questions about this section

Which service family covers the setup I need?

Front-entry and seasonal work sits under Seasonal Porch Decorating from $350+. Anything styled inside a home or hotel room sits under Celebration Setups from $300+. Table presentation for showers, corporate gatherings, and small events sits under Grazing Tables & Styled Setups from $250+.

Do the starting prices include take-down or food?

No. Takedown starts at $75, quoted with your setup. Base pricing covers styling only. Food is not included by default and can be sourced for an additional fee.
