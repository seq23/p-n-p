FAQ

Party planning and decor questions

What is included in a party setup?

Birthdays, anniversaries, proposals, baby and bridal showers, welcome-home surprises, hotel-room surprises and parties with a grazing table. Balloon arches and garlands, backdrops, rose petals and candles, table styling and signs.

Do you decorate hotel rooms?

Yes, and homes and venues too. For a hotel we coordinate access with the front desk or with you; for a venue we need their setup window.

How far ahead should I book?

Two to three weeks ahead is comfortable for most birthdays, and earlier for weekends in spring and December.

How long does setup take?

One to three hours depending on the setup. We arrive early enough to be gone before guests, or before your person walks in.

Do you take it down afterwards?

You keep it, or we come back for takedown. Takedown starts at $75, quoted with your setup.

What area do you serve?

Memphis, Germantown, Collierville, Bartlett, Lakeland, Arlington, Southaven and Olive Branch are included. Anywhere further is a flat $75 travel fee, shown on your quote up front.

How do I pay?

Your date is held by 50% of the quote, paid by Zelle or Cash App from your invoice. The rest is due by the time we arrive to set up. Zelle or Cash App only; we do not take cash or cards.
