What does a birthday hotel room for her look like?

Pick her colors and we build around them. The looks we are asked for most:

  • Pink, white and gold: soft, bright and great in photos.
  • Red and black: a romantic room with a birthday banner and her number in gold.
  • All white with florals: clean and elegant.
  • Her favorite: send a Pinterest link and we match it.

What is included?

Our Basic setup includes 15 ceiling balloons, banner, petal walkway, bed design, 8–12 LED tea lights. Luxury adds a styled balloon display, more candles and personal touches; Signature is the full room, with a balloon installation she can stand in front of for photos, a balloon ceiling and florals.

How do I make it photo-ready?

Give her one wall for pictures. A balloon arch or garland over the headboard with her number and a banner makes the shot; candles and petals on the bed fill the frame. Tell us where the window and the bed are once you have the room and we plan the photo spot.

When do you set up, and how does she not find out?

We are usually at the hotel between 3 and 6 PM, around check-in time, and gone before your person walks in. You check in, give the front desk our name or meet us at the room, and take her out while we work. We text you when it is ready. No hotel booked yet? Leave the hotel blank on the request form and text it to us once you have it.

We use LED candles only, never an open flame, so nothing sets off a smoke detector or breaks a hotel rule. Takedown is optional: for a hotel room it is $75, and it is included with Signature.

FAQ

What do people ask about a birthday hotel room for her?

How much are birthday hotel room decorations for her?

Basic starts at $300, Luxury at $500 and Signature at $1,500. These are starting prices; the exact quote depends on the room and how full you want it.

Can you add flowers?

Yes. Florals come with Signature, and we can add a bouquet to any setup. Tell us her favorite flowers and colors on the request form.

Do you use rose petals and candles?

Yes: rose petals on the bed and a walkway, and LED candles only. We never use an open flame in a hotel room.

When do you set up?

Usually between 3 and 6 PM, around check-in. Most rooms take one to three hours, and we are gone before she walks in.

Do I need to book the hotel first?

No. Leave the hotel blank on the request form and text it to us once you have it. We decorate at many local hotels, from mid-range to luxury.
