How many rose petals do you need for a hotel room?

For a hotel room, plan on roughly 150 to 200 rose petals for a filled heart on the bed, 400 to 600 to scatter the whole bed, and about 15 for every foot of walkway. These are planning estimates worked out from petal size, not a rule: how thickly you lay them changes the count.

How many petals for each part of the room?

Count by area. A rose petal is about 1.5 to 2 inches across, so a single overlapping layer takes about 30 to 40 petals per square foot and a loose scatter about 10 to 15. Worked out for a standard hotel room:

WherePetals (estimate)
Filled heart on the bed, about 3 feet wide150 to 200
Whole queen or king bed, loosely scattered400 to 600
Walkway, door to bed (10 to 15 feet)150 to 250
Tub, nightstands and a few extrasabout 100
Heart, walkway and extras togetherabout 400 to 550

Buy about a quarter more than you think. Petals clump in the bag and you will want to fill gaps.

Real or faux rose petals for a hotel room?

Faux silk petals are the safer choice in a hotel. Fresh red petals can bleed color onto white sheets and carpet, and a stained duvet is how a cleaning fee happens. Faux petals do not wilt before you get back to the room, and they lift off in a minute. If you want fresh petals, keep them on top of a throw or on the floor, not straight on the sheets.

How do you lay rose petals so they look full?

  1. Outline the shape first (a heart, initials or a path) with single petals.
  2. Fill the outline from the edge in, overlapping slightly.
  3. Scatter a few loose petals outside the shape so it looks natural.
  4. Do the bed last, after the balloons and candles are placed, so nothing gets knocked.

Frequently asked questions

How many rose petals do I need for a heart on the bed?

Roughly 150 to 200 petals for a filled heart about three feet wide, laid in a single slightly overlapping layer. A bigger heart, or a thicker layer, needs more. Buy about a quarter extra to fill gaps.

How many petals come in a bag?

It varies by brand. Faux silk petal bags are commonly sold by the hundred or by the thousand, so check the count on the bag rather than the weight or the size of the bag.

Will rose petals stain hotel sheets?

Fresh red petals can, especially on white sheets that get warm or damp. Faux silk petals do not stain. If you use fresh petals, put them on a throw or keep them to the floor and the nightstands.
