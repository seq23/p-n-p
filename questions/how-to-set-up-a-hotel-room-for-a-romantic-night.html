How to set up a hotel room for a romantic night

The way to set up a hotel room for a romantic night is to check in early, clear the surfaces, put balloons up first, then LED candles, then rose petals on the bed and a path from the door, and dim the lights. Plan on one to three hours, and finish before your person arrives.

What do you need to set up a romantic hotel room?

  • Rose petals (faux silk is safest on hotel sheets)
  • LED tea lights or pillar candles
  • Balloons and a way to hold them: weights, ribbon or a garland strip
  • A banner or sign
  • Flowers, a bottle or a gift for the nightstand
  • Scissors, spare batteries and a bag for the empty packaging

What order do you set up the room in?

  1. Check in and clear: move the hotel's clutter off the nightstands and dresser.
  2. Balloons first: the ceiling and the headboard, while the floor is still clear.
  3. Sign and flowers: where they will be in the photo.
  4. LED candles: nightstands, dresser, tub ledge and along the path.
  5. Petals last: the heart on the bed, then the path from the door.
  6. Lights: switch on the candles, turn off the overheads, leave one lamp low.

How long does it take?

One to three hours, depending on how much you are doing. Check-in is usually mid-afternoon, so most setups happen between 3 and 6 PM. Leave yourself time to pack the trash and get out before your person arrives.

What should you avoid?

  • Open flames: most hotels do not allow them and they can set off the smoke detector. Use LED candles.
  • Fresh petals straight on white sheets: they can stain.
  • Tape on walls, confetti and glitter: the usual causes of a cleaning fee.

Frequently asked questions

How early should I check in to set up the room?

As early as the hotel allows. Check-in is usually mid-afternoon, and a full setup takes one to three hours, so a 3 PM check-in gives you time before dinner.

Can I decorate a hotel room without the person knowing?

Yes. Check in, set up, then meet them somewhere else and come back together. Or have a decorator set up while you are both out; we text you when it is ready.

What is the easiest romantic hotel room setup?

Petals on the bed in a heart, LED tea lights on every surface, a few balloons over the headboard and the overhead lights off. That alone transforms a standard room.
