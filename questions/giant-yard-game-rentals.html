Giant Yard Game Rentals in Memphis

Giant yard game rentals are oversized versions of classic tabletop games designed for outdoor or large indoor spaces. These rentals include favorites like wooden stacking towers and large-scale four-in-a-row sets. They provide interactive entertainment for guests of all ages at Memphis backyard birthdays, office parties, and community gatherings.

How do I rent giant games for my Memphis event?

Planning a celebration in Germantown or Collierville often requires activities that keep guests moving and talking. Giant yard games serve as the perfect icebreaker for any crowd. You can choose from a variety of popular options that turn a standard lawn into a playground. Our collection features sturdy, well-maintained sets that stand up to enthusiastic play. These games are ideal for wedding rehearsals, family reunions, or corporate team-building days in the Mid-South. You simply select the items that fit your party theme and prepare for a day of friendly competition.

The process is straightforward for local hosts. Once you decide on your event date, you can secure your favorite games through our booking system. We focus on providing high-quality wooden and durable plastic sets that look great in photos and function perfectly. Whether you are hosting a small gathering in Lakeland or a large bash in Southaven, these games add a tactile, nostalgic element to the festivities. They work well on grass, pavement, or even inside a large office lobby during the holiday season.

To keep things simple and efficient for Memphis families, all game rentals are handled via pickup. Our central location at Mt. If you are looking for a complete event solution, you can pair these rentals with our other services. Frequently asked questions

Do you deliver the giant yard games to my house?

No, all game rentals are pickup only. You can collect your items at Mt. Moriah Station. This allows you to transport them to your location in Germantown, Collierville, or Southaven at your convenience. Please ensure your vehicle has enough space for oversized wooden sets and boards.

Can I use the yard games if it rains in Memphis?

Most of our games are made of wood or plastic and can handle light moisture. However, we recommend moving them indoors or under a covered porch during heavy rain to protect the materials. If your outdoor plans change, these games work great in garages or large living rooms.

How long is the rental period for the games?

The specific duration of your rental will be outlined in your custom quote. We work with you to ensure you have the games for the full length of your birthday party, shower, or corporate event. You simply pick them up before the fun starts and return them afterward.

Are the games difficult to set up by myself?

Not at all. Our giant yard games are selected for their simplicity. Most require no tools and can be ready for play in less than a minute. We will show you how they work during pickup at Mt. Moriah Station so you feel confident setting them up.
