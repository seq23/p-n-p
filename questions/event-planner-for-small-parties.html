Event planner for small parties in Memphis

An event planner for a small party is someone who plans how the party will look and sets it up, so the host does not have to. For birthdays, showers, hotel room surprises and office parties around Memphis, Porch & Party plans the decor with you, brings every piece and sets it up on the day.

Published November 9, 2026 · Updated November 22, 2026

What a small-party planner does

A small party still has a lot of parts. Someone has to choose the colors, buy the decorations, set the table, hang the banner and clear it all away afterward. When you are also the host, most of that lands on the morning of the party.

We take the decor side of that job. You tell us the date, the place and the kind of party. We plan the look with you, bring everything and set it up before your guests arrive. You keep the parts you enjoy, like the guest list and the playlist.

Small parties are a big share of our work: a birthday in a living room, a shower in a friend's backyard, a hotel room set up as a surprise, a holiday party in an office break room.

  • A birthday at home, from balloons to the cake table
  • A baby or bridal shower
  • A surprise in a hotel room
  • A holiday party or lobby display at the office

What we plan and set up

Porch & Party has four services, and one party can use more than one of them. Everything except the game rentals comes to you: our team brings it to your home, hotel room or office and sets it up there.

  • Celebration setups: balloons, banners, table decor and room styling
  • Grazing tables and styled decor for showers and office gatherings
  • Seasonal porch decorating, so the house looks ready from the street
  • Giant yard game rentals, which you pick up and return at Mt. Moriah Station

How planning with us works

First, use the quote button at the end of this page and tell us the date, the address, about how many guests are coming, and the colors or theme you have in mind. No theme yet? Pick Freestyle and we will choose one for you.

Next, we reply with an exact quote for your date and answer any questions about the look. A hotel that is not booked yet is fine; add the address when you have it.

On the day, our team arrives ahead of your guests and sets everything up. In hotel rooms we usually come in the afternoon around check-in, and we use LED candles only.

Small parties at the office

Office parties come with their own limits: a short window before people arrive, a break room or lobby that is in use all day, and a building with its own rules about what can go on the walls.

Tell us when the room is free and what the building allows, and we will plan the setup around it. Holiday lobby decor works the same way: pick the date and we set it up for the season. If you want food as well, a grazing table lets people help themselves between meetings.

Two things to plan around on the day

  • Never leave perishable food out for more than 2 hours, or 1 hour when it is above 90°F, the CDC says. (CDC)
  • Keep lit candles at least 12 inches from anything that can burn, the NFPA says. (NFPA)

Frequently asked questions

Do you plan parties for just a few people?

Yes. Many of our setups are for small parties at home, in a hotel room or at the office. A small party gets the same care as a big one: we agree on the look with you, bring every piece on the day and set it up before your guests arrive.

What areas do you serve?

We work across the Memphis area, including Germantown, Collierville, Lakeland, Southaven and Olive Branch. If your party is nearby but not on that list, send the request anyway with the address, and we will tell you in our reply whether we can get there.

Are party games delivered?

We do not deliver party game rentals. You book them online, see what is free on your date, collect them at Mt. Moriah Station before the party and return them there afterward. It is only the decor setups where our team comes to your home, hotel or office.

What goes on a grazing table?

That is up to you. We plan the menu together, from everyday choices to premium cheeses, cured meats and specialty fruit, and quote the food once the menu and head count are set. Then we lay it out and style the table so guests can help themselves as soon as they arrive.
