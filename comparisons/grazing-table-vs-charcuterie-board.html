Grazing table vs charcuterie board: what is the difference?

The two words get used interchangeably, and they describe different things. A charcuterie board is a serving board. A grazing table is a styled spread that takes over a table, and the difference changes who builds it, how many people it serves, and what you are actually paying for.

What this page recommends

A grazing table is a whole table styled with food laid directly across it, built on site to feed and anchor a larger gathering. Grazing table, defined

A grazing table is a food display that covers a table surface rather than a board, laid out and styled on site so guests serve themselves over the course of an event. Because it is built in place and sized to the table and the guest count, the setup and styling are as much a part of it as the food.

What this means in practice

The practical test is the guest count and the table. If a few people are sharing food at a coffee table, a board is the right tool and a grazing table is overkill. Once the food has to feed a room and hold the eye for the length of an event, a board gets picked over in the first half hour and a styled table is the better purchase.
