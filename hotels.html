01 / DOWNTOWN / THE PYRAMID

Big Cypress Lodge

1 Bass Pro Drive · Memphis, TN

BEST FOR: dramatic suite + bath

ROOM TO REQUEST

Treehouse Suite or King Cabin with air/jetted tub

WHY IT WORKS

Immersive rustic luxury inside the Pyramid: fireplaces, warm timber finishes and select rooms with private air tubs. Ideal when the ROOM itself should feel like the occasion.

PRICE GUIDANCE

From $246/night in a Nov 8-9 third-party example; other listings from $299. Room upgrades extra.

02 / DOWNTOWN / HISTORIC CORE

The Peabody Memphis

149 Union Avenue · Memphis, TN

BEST FOR: classic anniversary luxury

ROOM TO REQUEST

Premium King, Junior Suite, Brinkley or Celebrity Suite

WHY IT WORKS

Memphis landmark luxury with elegant guest rooms, classic lobby and on-site dining. The higher-tier suites are a special-occasion splurge.

PRICE GUIDANCE

Official posted rates: traditional rooms from $199; Premium King from $294; Junior Suite from $630; Brinkley from $799; Celebrity from $1,219.

03 / HARBOR TOWN / NEAR DOWNTOWN

River Inn of Harbor Town

50 Harbor Town Square · Memphis, TN

BEST FOR: peaceful waterfront escape

ROOM TO REQUEST

Superior King or select river-view room / suite

WHY IT WORKS

Quiet, refined riverfront boutique atmosphere with four-poster beds, intimate dining and sunset potential. Request a river-facing room explicitly.

PRICE GUIDANCE

Example $261/night base; $308/night with taxes/fees for Dec 5-6 on third-party listing.

04 / VICTORIAN VILLAGE / NEAR DOWNTOWN

The James Lee House

690 Adams Avenue · Memphis, TN

BEST FOR: romantic private bathtub

ROOM TO REQUEST

The Lee Suite (freestanding tub) or Goyer Suite (clawfoot tub)

WHY IT WORKS

Gorgeous restored Victorian mansion with distinctive suites. The Lee Suite has a standalone soaking tub in a bright tower bathroom; Goyer features a historic clawfoot tub.

PRICE GUIDANCE

Date-specific quote required; no dependable public base rate confirmed.
