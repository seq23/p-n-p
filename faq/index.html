Questions asked before booking

Do grazing tables include food?

Grazing-table pricing can sound straightforward until people realize some businesses are quoting styling only while others are quoting food, styling, and labor together.

Do party decorators come to your house?

A lot of people assume party decoration only happens in rented venues, but home setups are one of the most common requests for birthdays and personal celebrations.

How far in advance should I book party decor?

Booking timing matters because the strongest setups depend on availability, prep time, and enough room to plan the space without forcing rushed decisions.

How long does it take to set up party decor?

Setup time depends less on the label of the event and more on the actual scope, layout, and how much needs to be styled before the moment starts.

How much does birthday decoration at home cost?

At-home birthday decoration pricing depends on how much of the home is being styled, how visual the setup needs to be, and whether the design is simple or more layered.

How much do grazing tables cost?

Grazing-table pricing can be confusing because some businesses price styling only while others bundle in food, labor, or both.

How much does hotel room decor cost in Memphis?

Hotel room decor pricing in Memphis usually depends on the scale of the setup, the materials used, the timeline, and whether the design is simple or more built out.
