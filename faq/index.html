Booking & payment

How a booking works, what holds your date, and how you pay. These apply to porches, party and room decor, grazing tables and rentals.

How do I book?

Fill in the quote form; it takes about three minutes. We reply within one business day with what is open on your date and the total. Nothing is held until the deposit is in.

What holds my date?

Porch, party and room decor and grazing tables: 50% of the quote. Rentals: a $50 refundable damage deposit plus 50% of the rental. Both are paid by Zelle or Cash App from your invoice.

When is the rest due?

Porch, decor and grazing: by the time we arrive to set up. Rentals: at pickup, by Zelle or Cash App on the spot.

Do you take cash or cards?

No. Zelle or Cash App only, so every payment has a record on both sides.

Can I cancel or move my date?

Cancel 48 hours or more before and the deposit comes back in full. Inside 48 hours the deposit is kept. Rain or a storm moves your date free within 30 days.

Why do you ask for a photo ID?

It confirms the person booking is the person we meet. It is used for that only and deleted after your event.

How far do you travel?

Memphis, Germantown, Collierville, Bartlett, Lakeland, Arlington, Southaven and Olive Branch are included. Anywhere further is a flat $75 travel fee, shown on your quote up front. Rentals are picked up and returned at Mt. Moriah Station, Memphis Police Department, so no travel applies to them.

Where do your prices start?

Seasonal Porch Decorating from $350+, Celebration Setups from $300+, Grazing Tables & Styled Setups from $250+, and party game rentals at $40 a game a day. The exact quote depends on size, date, location and materials.

Celebration Setups

Party & room decor

Birthdays, anniversaries, proposals, showers and hotel-room surprises from $300+, at your home, a hotel or a venue.

What do you set up?

Birthdays, anniversaries, proposals, baby and bridal showers, welcome-home surprises, hotel-room surprises and parties with a grazing table. Balloon arches and garlands, backdrops, rose petals and candles, table styling and signs.

Home, hotel or venue?

All three. For a hotel we coordinate access with the front desk or with you; for a venue we need their setup window.

How much does party or room decor cost?

From $300+. The total depends on the size of the room, the pieces (balloons, backdrop, florals) and how many tables. Tell us your budget range on the form and we design to it.

Can you match a theme or colors?

Yes. Send a photo or a Pinterest link with your request.

How long does setup take?

One to three hours depending on the setup. We arrive early enough to be gone before guests, or before your person walks in.

What happens afterwards?

You keep it, or we come back for takedown. Takedown starts at $75, quoted with your setup.

Do you do the food too?

Base pricing covers styling only. Food is not included by default and can be sourced for an additional fee. Or we style food you have ordered.

What is a grazing table?

A long spread of cheeses, charcuterie, fruit, breads, dips and sweets, styled so guests help themselves. Ours start at $250 for the styling of a board.

Is food included?

Base pricing covers styling only. Food is not included by default and can be sourced for an additional fee. Or we style food you have ordered. Your quote shows setup and styling on one line and food, if we source it, as a separate rough estimate.

How many people does a table feed?

As a snack with drinks: a board for 10 to 15, a small table for 20 to 30, a full table for 40 to 60, and each additional table for about 40 more. As the meal, roughly half: a board for 6 to 8, a small table for 12 to 15, a full table for 25 to 30.

How much does the styling cost?

Board from $250, small table (4 to 6 ft) from $400, full table (8 ft and up) from $650, each additional table from $500. Food is separate.

How much does the food cost?

Food is always a rough estimate, from everyday selections at the low end to premium selections (imported cheeses, cured meats, specialty fruit) at the high end, and is quoted exactly once your menu and headcount are confirmed. Setup and styling is priced separately and never added into the food estimate. As a rough guide, about $12 to $18 a head.

Allergies and dietary needs?

Tell us on the form. Nut-free, vegetarian and halal selections are normal, and we label the table.

How long does it keep?

About two to three hours out at room temperature. For longer events we set up in two waves.

Do you bring the table and boards?

We bring boards, risers and the styling. The table itself is yours or the venue’s; tell us the size.

Rentals

Party game rentals

Giant yard games, an inflatable movie screen and a card table, $40 a game a day, picked up and returned at Mt. Moriah Station, Memphis Police Department. The same answers are on the rentals page.

Where do I pick up?

At Mt. Moriah Station, Memphis Police Department, 2602 Mt. Moriah Rd, Memphis, TN 38115. It is a safe exchange zone, and you return the rentals there too. After payment, text or call 901-355-1518 to set your pickup time.

How do I pay?

A $50 refundable damage deposit plus 50% of the rental fee holds your date, paid by Zelle or Cash App from your invoice. The rest is paid at pickup by Zelle or Cash App on the spot; we do not take cash. The damage deposit is refunded when you return everything.

What do I need to bring?

Your government photo ID, with the same name as the invoice, and a car. Every game fits an SUV or a sedan with the seats down, and the movie screen fits a trunk.

How much do the games cost?

Every game is $40 a day or $60 for a weekend. The inflatable movie screen is $50 a day or $75 for a weekend. The card table with cup holders is $15 a day or $25 for a weekend. Bundles: Pick 3 $110, Pick 5 $175, Whole Yard $195, Movie Night $110, Game Night $85, The Whole Party $235 ($350 for a weekend). There is no minimum order.

How long is a rental?

A day rental is returned within 24 hours of pickup. A weekend rental is picked up Friday and returned Monday.

What happens if it rains?

If rain or a storm is forecast for your date, we reschedule the rental free within 30 days. Every game also works indoors, so many hosts simply move the games inside, and Grab the Mic is a tabletop game made for an indoor party.

Can we use the games indoors?

Yes. Every game works indoors on flat floor space; the Giant Connect Four stands 4 ft tall. The inflatable movie screen is outdoor only and needs a plug within 50 ft.

Do you have a projector?

Yes, we can include one with the movie screen. Just ask when you book; it's one projector, so ask early.

How far ahead should I book?

Two to three weeks ahead is safest for Saturdays and holiday weekends, because each item is limited to one or two units. Weekday and short-notice requests are fine to send; we confirm what is open for your date.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Cancel 48 hours or more before your pickup time for a full refund.

Are the games safe for kids, and who can rent?

The renter must be 18 or older and an adult should supervise play. Giant Color Stack is made for ages 6 and up, Giant Uno for 7 and up and Grab the Mic for 8 and up; every other game suits older kids, teens and adults. Games are wiped down between rentals.

Is this a sale?

No. Everything on the rentals page is rental only and comes back to us after your event.
