A styled gender reveal setup, defined

A styled gender reveal setup is decor designed around the reveal moment and installed on site: typically a backdrop or balloon installation, a styled table, and the focal point where the reveal is photographed. It differs from buying a themed kit in that the design, assembly, installation and placement are done for you and scaled to the actual space.

What this means in practice

The practical read: if the reveal is a small family moment and you have an afternoon free, a themed kit and some help will do it. If the reveal is being photographed or filmed for people who are not there, the backdrop is the thing everyone will see afterwards, and that is the part worth paying to have designed and installed properly.
