How much does a balloon garland cost in Memphis?

A balloon garland is the decor most people price first, because it is the part of a setup they can picture. The number splits sharply depending on which thing you are actually buying: a kit you inflate yourself, or an installed garland that arrives finished and hung.

Installed balloon garland, defined

An installed balloon garland is a continuous run of inflated balloons, built in graduated sizes and colours and secured to a wall, arch, table, or entry by the decorator on site. What separates it from a kit is that inflation, assembly, transport, and hanging are all done for you, and the garland is scaled and shaped to the specific space rather than to a bag of a fixed count.

What this means in practice

The practical read: if the garland is the whole decor and you have an afternoon free, a retail kit does the job. If the garland is the backdrop for photographs that outlive the party, or it is going into a hotel room you can only enter an hour before the guest does, the install is the thing you are paying for and not the balloons. Most requests that come in for "just a balloon garland" turn out, once described, to be a full setup with a garland in it.

Can I book just a balloon garland with nothing else?

You can ask. A garland on its own is a small scope, so whether it works as a standalone booking depends on the date, the venue, and what else is on the schedule. Describe the request in the quote form and Porch & Party will say quickly whether it fits.

Before you book

Before requesting a quote, know the wall or space the garland is going on, roughly how long a run you want, the colours, and the exact window when the room or venue is accessible. Access time changes more quotes than colour does.
