Professional Christmas decorating, defined

Professional Christmas decorating is a paid service where a decorator designs the seasonal look, sources or arranges the materials, and installs everything on site. It is distinct from Christmas light installation, which is an electrical hanging service priced by linear foot of roofline. Porch & Party does the first: seasonal porch and front-entry styling, not roofline light runs.

What this means in practice

In practice, the useful comparison is not "decorator versus free". Decorating a front porch yourself still means buying greenery, ribbon, lighting, and containers, and most of that spend repeats or gets replaced year to year. A quote at the $350 starting point buys the design decision, the sourcing, and an install that is done in one visit instead of across three weekends. Where hiring stops being worth it is a small stoop with decor you already like and a free Saturday.

Before requesting a quote, take one photo of your front entry in daylight, decide whether you want a fresh look or a restyle of decor you already own, and settle on whether you want take-down included. Those three answers are most of what decides the number.
