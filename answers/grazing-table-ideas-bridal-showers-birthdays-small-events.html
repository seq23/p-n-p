What are good grazing table ideas for bridal showers, birthdays, and small events?

Good grazing table ideas combine a clean layout, layered serving pieces, color direction, small floral or decor accents, signage when useful, and enough negative space for the table to look styled instead of crowded. Porch & Party can style grazing-table presentation for showers, birthdays, brunches, and small events.
