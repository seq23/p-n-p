Quick Answer

What is the difference between party decor and event planning?

Party decor focuses on how the space looks and feels: styling, setup, room presentation, table moments, and focal decor. Event planning usually includes broader vendor coordination, timeline management, logistics, and production. Porch & Party is decor and setup focused.
