Balloon garland vs full birthday setup

A balloon garland works when you need one focal decorative moment. A full birthday setup makes more sense when the event needs a stronger overall look with multiple styled elements or a more finished room transformation. A full setup usually gives stronger photo coverage because more of the space looks intentional, not just one focal corner.
