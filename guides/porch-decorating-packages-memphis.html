What a porch package is

A porch package is a starting size of look with a from-price, not a fixed kit. Not sure which one? A small stoop is usually our Stoop package, a standard porch our Classic, and a large porch or one with steps our Grand. Every porch is a custom design. Common questions

What does the Stoop porch package include?

A simple touch. Fall: 8–10 pumpkins and gourds, door accent. From $350. Christmas: Wreath and door accent. From $450.

What does the Classic porch package include?

A full porch. Fall: A fuller porch display: more pumpkins and gourds in mixed sizes, door accent, layered height. From $550. Christmas: Wreath, door garland and a pair of porch accents. From $750.

What does the Grand porch package include?

The porch and the steps. Fall: A large display across the porch and steps with a statement piece. From $750. Christmas: Garland across the door and railings, wreaths, lit accents, steps styled. From $1,050.

What does the Showstopper porch package include?

The works: porch, steps and walkway. Fall: Porch, steps and walkway, fully layered, our most dramatic look. From $1,200. Christmas: The full porch: lit garland everywhere, windows, statement pieces. From $1,600.

Which porch package do I need?

Not sure which one? A small stoop is usually our Stoop package, a standard porch our Classic, and a large porch or one with steps our Grand. If you are not sure, pick “Not sure yet, help me decide” on the request form.

Is there a discount for booking fall and Christmas?

Yes. Book fall and Christmas together and save 10% on your Christmas package.

Are add-ons included in the package price?

No. Want more? Ask about add-ons: takedown, extra pumpkins or accents, a Thanksgiving refresh, lighting, matching windows or a lamp post. Add-ons are priced with your quote.
