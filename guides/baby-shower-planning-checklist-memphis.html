Baby Shower Planning

Baby shower planning checklist for Memphis hosts

Most baby shower checklists are lists of things to buy. The order matters more than the list: two or three decisions gate everything else, and hosts who make them out of order end up rebooking around a venue or a date that was never confirmed. This checklist is ordered by what blocks what.

Why the order of a shower checklist matters more than the list

Every item on a shower checklist depends on something above it. Guest count decides venue; venue decides layout; layout decides what decor and food service actually fit. Buying decor before the venue is confirmed is the most common and most expensive mistake, because a backdrop bought for a living room rarely suits a rented room, and vice versa.

The baby shower planning checklist, in order

  1. Confirm the date window with the guest of honour. Showers usually land in the later stages of pregnancy but comfortably before the due date. Nothing else can be booked until this window is agreed, so this is step one and not a formality.
  2. Settle the guest list and get a real count. The count decides the venue, the food quantity, and the seating. An approximate count is enough to start, but a firm one is needed before anything is paid for.
  3. Choose and confirm the venue. A home, a hotel or venue room, or a private space at a restaurant. Confirm it in writing before moving on — this is the item that most often changes and invalidates the steps after it.
  4. Lock the date and time. Once the venue is confirmed, fix the date. Weekend daytime slots go first, so treat the venue and the date as one decision where you can.
  5. Send invitations with enough notice. Aim to give guests several weeks. Send earlier if the shower is on a holiday weekend or if guests are travelling into Memphis for it.
  6. Decide the theme or palette. Do this after the venue, not before. A palette chosen against a room you have seen holds up; one chosen in the abstract usually needs redoing.
  7. Plan the food and the drinks. Decide the format first — seated meal, light bites, or a grazing table — because format decides quantity, service, and how much table space the room has to give up.
  8. Book decor and setup. This is the right point, with the venue, count, and palette settled. Porch & Party celebration setups start at $300+ and grazing table styling starts at $250+; both are quoted on the space and the scope, so having those three answers ready makes the quote fast and accurate.
  9. Plan the games, activities, and favours. Keep them to what the room and the timeline allow. A crowded activity list is the thing hosts most often cut on the day.
  10. Confirm everything one week out. Re-confirm the venue, the final head count, the food order, and the setup access window. The access window is the detail most often left vague and most likely to cause trouble.
  11. Set the day-of timeline. Work backwards from the guest arrival time: setup access, decor install, food placement, then guests. Give the install more room than you think it needs.

If you have less notice than this sequence assumes, keep the order and compress the gaps. Reordering it is what causes the rework.

What this means in practice

The step hosts most often get wrong is the eighth one, and they get it wrong by doing it third. Decor booked before the venue is confirmed has to be reworked when the room turns out to be a different shape, a different colour, or available an hour later than expected. Booking it after the venue, the count, and the palette are settled is the difference between one quote and three.

How far in advance should a baby shower be planned?

Give yourself enough runway to confirm the venue and send invitations with several weeks of notice for guests. Shorter timelines are workable — the order of the checklist matters more than the total lead time, and decor requests can still be reviewed on short notice subject to availability.

Before you book

Before requesting a decor quote, have the venue confirmed, the guest count settled, the palette chosen, and the setup access window known. Those four answers are what turn a quote request into an accurate quote instead of a conversation.
