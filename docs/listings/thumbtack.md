## Introduction (Thumbtack "About your business")
Porch & Party is a Memphis decorating studio for seasonal front porches, hotel-room and at-home celebration setups, and styled grazing tables. We design the look, bring every piece, install it on site and leave the space ready for photos: fall and Christmas porches, birthday and anniversary hotel rooms, proposal and romantic setups, baby and bridal showers, and grazing tables for showers and corporate gatherings. Every quote is based on your date, location, scope and budget range, and we serve Memphis, Germantown, Collierville, Bartlett, Lakeland, Arlington, Southaven and Olive Branch.
