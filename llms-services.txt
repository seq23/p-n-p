One day: pick up the day before the event or that morning, return the day after or 2 days after. Weekend: pick up Thursday or Friday, return Monday or Tuesday. Same price whichever days. No minimum order.
- Giant Connect Four (4 ft tall, indoor or outdoor)
- Cornhole set, 2 regulation boards + 8 bags
